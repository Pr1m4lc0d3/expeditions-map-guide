Colour and textures

Terrain texture layers, tint maps, and getting the colour of a real place right.

This page is outline. It may be incomplete or unverified. Anything marked unverified has not been proven in a running game.

Planned for this page:

  • terrain texture layers and how they blend
  • tint maps, and separating colour from lightness. Two surfaces should usually differ in chroma, not only in brightness
  • a caution when matching a real place: satellite imagery providers disagree with each other substantially on hue, and ground photographs are a better reference than any single provider
  • road overlays carry their own colour, separately from the terrain layers
