Small RC drones

How to make a drone the size of an RC car that deploys from a truck, drives, and rides over mud without digging itself in. Four steps, two of them changes to the game files.

This page is draft. It may be incomplete or unverified. Anything marked unverified has not been proven in a running game.

Small RC drones#

Works, on PC, with a patched game. A drone about half the size of the stock scout drone, built to look like an RC crawler, deploys from a truck, drives, and rides over real mud without leaving a rut. That’s confirmed in game (2026-11-22). Two of the steps change the game’s own files, so a mod on mod.io can’t deliver it alone, and consoles can’t run it. The tools that apply those changes aren’t released yet.

A carried drone is two classes. The drone is a truck class with IsWheelDrone="true". The part that carries it is an add-on with HasWheelDrone="true" and WheelDroneType="<the drone's class name>". Build both, then work through the four steps below.

1. Let the game accept a drone from a mod#

The game decides what kind of drone it has by name. A script in initial.pak compares the name with exactly drone_anchor, drone_cargo and drone_scout. A class shipped in a mod loads under its own name plus the mod’s id (my_drone becomes my_drone_1234567), so it never matches. The drone is typed “none”, it won’t deploy, and the garage throws script errors. Renaming your class to drone_scout doesn’t help, because the id is still added.

Do this: change that comparison so any non-empty drone name counts as a scout. It’s a two-byte change to the script in initial.pak. Compare with the empty string, not with a name nothing uses. The second version also makes every ordinary add-on count as a scout, and the inventory bar fills with dozens of scout-drone tabs.

2. Use the stock drone’s wheel settings#

In the drone’s wheel block, use Torque="default", as the stock drones do. With Torque="full" the drone deploys and sits there, and the wheels never turn at any engine torque.

If a drone won’t drive and you can’t see why, bisect it. Copy the stock scout drone, which drives, change one thing from your drone, and test. The copy that stops driving names the cause.

3. Scale the stock drone, don’t invent one#

Start from the stock scout drone class and scale it by one factor k (0.5 for half size):

Setting Scaled by
positions (Pos, Origin, CenterOfMassOffset), SteeringJointOffset, wheel Scale and OffsetZ k
body and tyre mass k³
engine torque k⁴
gearbox AngVel 1 / k
suspension Height, SuspensionMin, SuspensionMax k
suspension Strength k²

Keep the body’s collision box about 6 cm above the tops of the tyres, or it grabs the tyres. Then put any body you like on top as the drawn mesh.

4. Stop the ruts#

At this point the drone drives, but on dirt, grass and mud it digs a trench and sinks. The cause is in Expeditions.exe. Three amounts in the game’s ground code are fixed sizes made for trucks, and nothing in a class file reaches them:

  • Mud sink. A tyre sinks half the painted mud’s thickness before the mud starts holding it up. That’s a few centimetres for any truck, and most of a small tyre.
  • Ground digging. Each contact on soft ground can lower the ground under the tyre by up to 15.6 cm.
  • The rut stamp. The mark pressed into mud is never smaller than 0.3 m wide and 0.4 m in radius, however small the tyre. Normal soil often carries a thin layer of mud you can’t see, so this fires even where the ground looks dry.

Do this: four small patches to Expeditions.exe that scale those amounts to the tyre. They act only on tyres under 0.33 m in radius (the stock drone’s tyre), so every truck and stock drone runs exactly as before.

  1. The mud sink shrinks with the tyre, by (radius ÷ 0.33)⁴.
  2. Small tyres never lower the ground layer.
  3. Small tyres get full mud support at a quarter of the sink, so they ride on thin mud.
  4. The rut stamp is drawn at the tyre’s own size.

With all four in, the drone drives a whole map without digging a rut and crosses real mud like a real RC car.

What’s still open#

  • A game update or an Epic “verify files” restores the original Expeditions.exe and initial.pak. The patches have to go back on after each one.
  • The drone shakes slightly when it sits still with the motor running.
  • The patch tools aren’t public yet. This page will link them when they are.
