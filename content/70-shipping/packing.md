Planned for this page:

- what the pack step produces, and why there are several files per map
- why it takes around 25 minutes: output is built for six platforms in sequence. A long
  pack is not a hang
- **what the pack rewrites**, which is the part that matters. Zone properties can come
  back empty
- the check to run every single time, before you spend half an hour testing in game
- distributing a finished map, and one thing never to do to the distribution archive:
  rebuild it with a general-purpose zip tool. The result stops being recognised
