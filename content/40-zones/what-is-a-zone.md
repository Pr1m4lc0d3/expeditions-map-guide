Read this before the other zone pages. A zone is two things that get edited separately,
and understanding the split makes everything else obvious.

Planned for this page:

- the **shape** is an object you place in the map editor, and it carries an **Id**
- the **behaviour** is a set of properties you attach in the zone settings tool
- the two are matched by that Id, character for character. This is where most zone
  problems actually come from
- one zone can carry several properties at once. A deploy zone that is also a store is
  a single zone with two properties
