Planned for this page:

- the storage property: capacity, what spawns, and whether it always spawns
- airdrops, and how their contents are randomised per expedition
- setting specific custom loot instead of random contents
- the Warehouse is the one storage whose contents survive between expeditions

!!! danger "Read the naming rule first"
    A zone whose name contains a **space** can never be looted, because the game splits
    its internal item reference on whitespace. This is covered in full on
    [Where everything lives](../00-start/where-things-live.html).
