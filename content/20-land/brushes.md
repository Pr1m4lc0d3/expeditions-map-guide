Every terrain brush has three controls, and the middle one does most of the work.

Planned for this page:

- **Size, Falloff and Strength** on every brush
- falloff 0 is hard: flat top, vertical sides. This is what builds benches and walls
- falloff 1 is a soft dome, which makes bumps
- the classic failure: using a soft brush to lay a road, which produces a line of domes
- the Geometry Smooth brush, what it actually filters, and the fact that it only cuts
- when the editor's hand tools are right, and when a script is genuinely the only
  option. A bench that holds a constant grade down a hillside has no hand tool
