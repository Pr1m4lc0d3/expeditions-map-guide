Planned for this page:

- River and water objects, and how a channel gets its shape
- foam and spray, including the mistake that looks wrong immediately: foam computed
  evenly along a river comes out as mirror-symmetric bands. Real foam starts at a
  discrete obstruction and streaks downstream
- mist and spray are placeable models, not an effect setting
- if you are importing real terrain, **water is never in elevation data.** Survey
  specifications flatten any waterbody above a couple of acres to a plane. Valleys and
  banks are captured, beds are not
